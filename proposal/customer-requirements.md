# 3. Customer Requirements

## 3.1 Requirements Overview

NorthStar Maritime Services requires a centralized solution that supports maritime operational monitoring, mission coordination, secure communication, reporting, system oversight, and supporting documentation.

The following requirements have been identified for the proposed Maritime Mission Support System (MMSS).

## 3.2 Requirements Table

| Requirement ID | Customer Requirement | Priority | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| MMSS-001 | Provide a centralized operational dashboard. | High | Authorized users can view current operational information from a centralized dashboard. |
| MMSS-002 | Display vessel location and operational status. | High | Authorized users can view the location and current status of monitored vessels. |
| MMSS-003 | Provide secure communication between authorized users. | High | Authorized users can exchange operational information through controlled communication channels. |
| MMSS-004 | Provide role-based access control. | High | Users can access system functions and information according to their assigned roles. |
| MMSS-005 | Maintain an audit trail of significant system activities. | Medium | Significant user and system activities are recorded and available to authorized personnel for review. |
| MMSS-006 | Provide operational reports and summaries. | Medium | Authorized users can generate or access defined operational reports and summaries. |
| MMSS-007 | Provide user and system documentation. | High | User, administrative, technical, and operational documentation is available to support system use and maintenance. |
| MMSS-008 | Provide system monitoring and maintenance procedures. | High | Documented procedures define monitoring activities, troubleshooting steps, maintenance tasks, and escalation processes. |

## 3.3 Requirement Details

### MMSS-001 — Centralized Operational Dashboard

**Requirement:**  
The system shall provide a centralized operational dashboard for authorized personnel.

**Purpose:**  
Provide users with a consolidated view of important operational information without requiring them to access multiple system areas.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Dashboard is accessible to authorized users.
- Operational information is presented in a structured format.
- Dashboard information supports operational monitoring.
- Access is controlled according to user role.

**Proposed Response:**  
The MMSS operational dashboard will provide a centralized view of vessel status, mission information, system events, and other relevant operational information.

---

### MMSS-002 — Vessel Location and Status

**Requirement:**  
The system shall provide authorized users with vessel location and operational status information.

**Purpose:**  
Support operational awareness and mission coordination.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Vessel information is available to authorized users.
- Vessel location can be displayed.
- Current operational status can be displayed.
- Access to vessel information follows defined permissions.

**Proposed Response:**  
The MMSS vessel monitoring capability will provide authorized users with access to vessel location and status information through the operational dashboard.

---

### MMSS-003 — Secure Communications

**Requirement:**  
The system shall support controlled communication between authorized users.

**Purpose:**  
Provide an approved mechanism for exchanging operational information.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Communication functions are restricted to authorized users.
- Access is controlled according to user permissions.
- Communication activity can be monitored or audited where required.

**Proposed Response:**  
MMSS will incorporate controlled communication services that allow authorized personnel to exchange operational information.

---

### MMSS-004 — Role-Based Access Control

**Requirement:**  
The system shall provide role-based access control.

**Purpose:**  
Ensure that users can access only the system functions and information required for their responsibilities.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Defined user roles are supported.
- Permissions can be assigned according to user roles.
- Restricted functions cannot be accessed by unauthorized users.
- Administrative procedures define user access management.

**Proposed Response:**  
The MMSS access-control model will use defined user roles and permissions to manage access to system functions and operational information.

---

### MMSS-005 — Audit Trail

**Requirement:**  
The system shall maintain an audit trail of significant system activities.

**Purpose:**  
Support accountability, troubleshooting, security review, and operational oversight.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Significant system activities are recorded.
- Audit information is available to authorized personnel.
- Audit information can support operational review and investigation.

**Proposed Response:**  
MMSS will provide audit logging for significant user and system activities. Administrative procedures will define appropriate review and retention practices.

---

### MMSS-006 — Operational Reporting

**Requirement:**  
The system shall provide operational reports and summaries.

**Purpose:**  
Support operational monitoring, analysis, and decision-making.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Defined operational reports are available.
- Reports present information in a structured format.
- Access to reports is controlled according to user roles.

**Proposed Response:**  
The MMSS reporting capability will provide authorized users with operational summaries, vessel status reports, mission information, system activity reports, and maintenance-related reports.

---

### MMSS-007 — Documentation

**Requirement:**  
The solution shall provide documentation required to support system use, administration, troubleshooting, and maintenance.

**Purpose:**  
Ensure that users and technical personnel have consistent guidance for operating and maintaining the solution.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- User documentation is available.
- Administrative documentation is available.
- Technical documentation is available.
- Troubleshooting and maintenance procedures are documented.
- Documentation is maintained under controlled revision practices.

**Proposed Response:**  
A structured documentation set will be developed as part of the MMSS implementation. Documentation will use standardized templates and controlled revision practices.

---

### MMSS-008 — Monitoring and Maintenance Procedures

**Requirement:**  
The solution shall provide documented procedures for system monitoring and maintenance.

**Purpose:**  
Support reliable system operation and provide technical personnel with consistent processes for identifying and resolving issues.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- System monitoring procedures are documented.
- Maintenance procedures are documented.
- Troubleshooting guidance is available.
- Escalation procedures are defined.
- Procedures identify appropriate responsibilities.

**Proposed Response:**  
MMSS will include operational monitoring, troubleshooting, and maintenance procedures covering system health checks, issue identification, maintenance activities, and escalation.

## 3.4 Requirements Prioritization

Requirements have been categorized according to their relative importance to the proposed solution.

### High Priority

- MMSS-001 — Centralized operational dashboard
- MMSS-002 — Vessel location and status
- MMSS-003 — Secure communications
- MMSS-004 — Role-based access control
- MMSS-007 — Documentation
- MMSS-008 — Monitoring and maintenance procedures

### Medium Priority

- MMSS-005 — Audit trail
- MMSS-006 — Operational reporting

Requirement priorities may be reviewed and adjusted during project planning based on customer feedback, technical dependencies, implementation constraints, and approved scope changes.

Each requirement will be traced through the proposal and implementation documentation to demonstrate how customer needs are addressed.

The requirements will subsequently be mapped to:

- Proposed technical solution
- Compliance matrix
- Requirements traceability matrix
- System architecture
- Implementation activities
- Documentation deliverables
- Verification activities
