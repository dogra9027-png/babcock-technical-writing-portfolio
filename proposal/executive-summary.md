# 1. Executive Summary

NorthStar Maritime Services requires a reliable and centralized solution for monitoring maritime operations, coordinating missions, and providing authorized personnel with timely operational information.

The proposed **Maritime Mission Support System (MMSS)** is designed to address these requirements through a centralized operational platform that brings together vessel status, mission information, secure communications, reporting, and operational monitoring.

The proposed solution provides role-based access to operational information, enabling personnel to access information according to their responsibilities. A centralized dashboard provides an overview of active vessels and mission activities, while reporting capabilities support operational analysis and decision-making.

MMSS also incorporates audit logging and monitoring capabilities to support accountability, system oversight, and operational maintenance. Supporting documentation, including user guidance, operational procedures, and system documentation, will be developed as part of the proposed solution.

The implementation approach emphasizes controlled deployment, user readiness, documentation, and ongoing support. The proposed solution is intended to provide NorthStar Maritime Services with a structured and maintainable platform that supports operational visibility while establishing clear processes for system use, monitoring, and maintenance.
