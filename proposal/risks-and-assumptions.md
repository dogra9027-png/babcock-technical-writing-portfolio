# 8. Risks and Assumptions

## 8.1 Purpose

This section identifies key assumptions, risks, dependencies, and constraints associated with the proposed Maritime Mission Support System (MMSS).

The purpose is to provide transparency around factors that may affect solution design, implementation, documentation, schedule, or operational readiness.

Risks and assumptions should be reviewed throughout the project lifecycle and updated when new information becomes available.

## 8.2 Key Assumptions

The proposal is based on the following assumptions:

| ID | Assumption | Potential Impact |
|---|---|---|
| ASM-001 | NorthStar Maritime Services will provide timely access to required subject matter experts. | Delayed requirements clarification or technical review if SMEs are unavailable. |
| ASM-002 | Customer requirements provided during project initiation represent the initial approved scope. | Changes to requirements may affect schedule, resources, and deliverables. |
| ASM-003 | Required operational data will be available through approved interfaces or data sources. | Data availability may affect system functionality and integration activities. |
| ASM-004 | Authorized users will receive appropriate system access before operational testing begins. | Testing and validation activities may be delayed if access is unavailable. |
| ASM-005 | Customer stakeholders will participate in scheduled reviews and approvals. | Delayed reviews may affect documentation and project milestones. |
| ASM-006 | Required technical infrastructure will be available before deployment activities begin. | Infrastructure delays may affect configuration and deployment. |
| ASM-007 | Customer documentation standards and templates will be provided during project initiation where applicable. | Additional effort may be required if project-specific standards are introduced later. |

## 8.3 Project Risks

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSK-001 | Requirements may change after solution design has started. | Medium | High | Maintain requirements traceability and apply controlled change management. |
| RSK-002 | SME availability may be limited during technical reviews. | Medium | Medium | Schedule SME reviews in advance and identify alternate reviewers where appropriate. |
| RSK-003 | Technical integration requirements may be more complex than initially anticipated. | Medium | High | Conduct early technical assessment and validate interfaces during the design phase. |
| RSK-004 | Documentation reviews may take longer than planned. | Medium | Medium | Establish review timelines and track review comments to closure. |
| RSK-005 | Incomplete technical information may delay documentation development. | Medium | Medium | Identify information gaps early and maintain an open-items list for SME follow-up. |
| RSK-006 | Deployment issues may affect operational readiness. | Low | High | Conduct deployment readiness reviews and maintain rollback and support procedures. |
| RSK-007 | Changes to system functionality may require updates to approved documentation. | Medium | Medium | Establish documentation impact assessment as part of change management. |
| RSK-008 | Operational users may require additional training following deployment. | Medium | Medium | Gather user feedback and provide supplementary guidance or training where required. |

## 8.4 Risk Rating

Risks will be evaluated using likelihood and impact.

| Rating | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Limited effect on project objectives and can normally be managed through routine project activities. |
| **Medium** | May affect schedule, resources, or deliverables and requires active monitoring. |
| **High** | May significantly affect project objectives and requires defined mitigation and management attention. |
